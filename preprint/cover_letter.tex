\begin{letter}{Editors-in-Chief\\
\textit{Linear Algebra and its Applications}}

\opening{Dear Editors-in-Chief,}

Please consider my manuscript, ``Permanental Dominance for Generalized
Matrix Functions of Order Four,'' for publication as a regular article in
\textit{Linear Algebra and its Applications}.

The manuscript proves the complete order-four case of the general subgroup
permanental-dominance conjecture.  More precisely, for every subgroup
$H\leq S_4$, every irreducible complex character $\chi$ of $H$, and every
$4\times4$ Hermitian positive-semidefinite matrix $A$, it establishes
\[
  \frac{d_\chi^H(A)}{\chi(1)}\leq \operatorname{per} A.
\]
This goes beyond the usual $S_4$ immanant specialization by treating all
thirty-seven irreducible-character cases arising from the eleven conjugacy
classes of subgroups of $S_4$.

The main conceptual contribution is the treatment of the two non-real
$A_4$ characters.  Their inequalities are reduced to polynomial
nonnegativity on a cone of $3\times3$ positive-semidefinite Gram matrices and
are proved using a rank-one sum-of-squares identity, an exact interior
positive-definiteness certificate, rational Gram certificates, and closure.
The remaining thirty-five cases are resolved by general principal-minor,
moment, and block-contraction inequalities.  A companion Lean formalization
provides machine-checked counterparts of the central identities and the
complete finite reduction.

The result and its methods lie directly within the algebraic, combinatorial,
and geometric aspects of matrix theory covered by the journal.  The
manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under
consideration by another journal.  A preprint and the companion formalization
are publicly available in the cited repository.  No external funding was
received, and I have no competing interests to declare.

Thank you for considering the manuscript.

\closing{Sincerely,}
